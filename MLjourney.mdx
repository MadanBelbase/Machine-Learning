# 200 Days Machine Learning Roadmap: From Zero to Advanced

This roadmap is tailored for absolute beginners (zero-level) aspiring to pursue ML research in the future. It spans 200 days, assuming 1-2.5 hours of daily study/practice (adjust based on your 1.2-2.5 hour availability; focus on consistency). It combines free resources (YouTube, Kaggle, arXiv, etc.) with DataCamp courses (accessible via your student account) for interactive learning. To build research skills early, papers and reproduction exercises are integrated progressively, starting lightly and building to advanced research methods, paper critiques, and experiment design. This prepares you for future ML research (e.g., publishing in NeurIPS/ICML, grad school apps)

**Key Guidelines:**
- **Daily Structure:** 
  - **Theory:** Mix of free videos/readings and DataCamp (student access) for hands-on.
  - **Practice:** Coding in Colab/Kaggle/DataCamp exercises; reproduce concepts for research mindset.
  - **Resources:** Both free and DataCamp; links provided. Focus on understanding "why" for research.
- **Tools Needed:** Colab (free), GitHub (version control), DataCamp student account. Track in repo (`MadanBelbase/ML-200-Days-Challenge`).
- **Git Tips (from your error):** Always `git pull origin main` before edits; resolve conflicts, then `git add`, `git commit`, `git push`.
- **Tracking Progress:** Daily GitHub commits. Join r/MachineLearning, ML Discord. Build a research journal (notebook summaries, paper notes).
- **Research Emphasis:** Early intro to papers (abstracts), building to critiques, reproductions, experiment design. End with research proposal and publishing basics.
- **Pacing:** If time-constrained (1.2 hours), prioritize theory; extend to 2.5 for practice/papers. Breaks every 30 days.
