This paper introduces a general end-to-end approach for mapping input sequences to output sequences using Deep Neural Networks. The researchers utilized a multilayered Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) architecture to compress a source sentence into a fixed-dimensional vector, which a second LSTM then decodes into a target translation. A major technical discovery revealed that reversing the order of words in the source sentences significantly boosts performance by creating shorter-term dependencies for the model to learn. Their system achieved impressive results on English to French translation, outperforming traditional phrase-based statistical models on large-scale tasks. Furthermore, the model proved surprisingly effective at maintaining accuracy when processing exceptionally long sentences.
